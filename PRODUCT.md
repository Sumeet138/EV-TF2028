# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary (inferred from the problem statement): planners at MHI, ICAT and state transport or energy departments, deciding where public EV charging money goes next. They work on a laptop or a projected screen in a review meeting, and they must defend each number to someone senior.

Secondary: the Techfest 2026-27 judges (IIT Bombay, MHI, ICAT), who see the product in a short demo.

## Product Purpose

EV Infra Map India puts public chargers, VAHAN registrations, roads, grid and demographics on one map. It shows where charging lags EV adoption and estimates how many chargers each district and corridor needs next.

Success: a planner picks a state, sees its worst gaps, and leaves with a defensible charger count, a type mix and candidate sites, plus the assumptions behind them.

## Positioning

The only view that joins operator charger locations to VAHAN demand at district level and says openly which numbers are measured and which are modelled.

## Evidence Rules

- VAHAN data is state-level. District EV counts are modelled and must be labelled as modelled wherever they appear.
- The charger inventory covers part of the official national total. The coverage share must stay visible, and a gap must never read as a proven absence of chargers.
- Every assumption is listed in the product and in the methodology document.

## Accessibility

WCAG AA contrast. Full keyboard use of every control. Choropleth meaning is never carried by colour alone: values are readable in text and tables.

## Voice

Plain, specific, numerate. Short sentences in the language of planners: kW, connectors, districts, corridors. Some wit is welcome in empty states and small moments, never in a number or a recommendation.
