# Problem Statement

Participants choose one of two challenges. **We are doing Challenge 1.**

## Challenge 1: EV Infra Map India

**Mapping and Integration of Nationwide EV Charger Installations Based on Vehicle Population Using VAHAN Data**

India's charging infrastructure is expanding through the efforts of government agencies, public-sector entities, charge point operators, utilities, and private companies. However, information on installed chargers is distributed across multiple applications and databases. This fragmentation limits the ability to develop a unified view of charging availability and assess whether infrastructure deployment is aligned with EV adoption across regions.

## Objective

Develop a data-driven national platform that:

- Integrates and maps existing public EV charging infrastructure across India.
- Uses VAHAN vehicle registration data to assess the relationship between EV population and charging availability.
- Identifies underserved areas and charging infrastructure gaps.
- Estimates future charging infrastructure requirements across different regions.
- Supports evidence-based planning by MHI, ICAT, state governments, and other relevant stakeholders.

## Data Layers to Integrate

| Data Layer | Illustrative Sources |
|---|---|
| EV population & vehicle registration | VAHAN database and state transport data |
| Existing EV charging infrastructure | Public charging applications, charger networks and open databases |
| Geographic & administrative boundaries | GIS platforms and open mapping sources |
| Road and mobility network | National highways, major roads and transport corridors |
| Demographic and economic activity | Population, urbanisation and commercial activity datasets |
| Electricity infrastructure | Publicly available grid and utility information |
| Supporting contextual information | Tourism, logistics, land-use and urban development data |

Where live or detailed datasets are unavailable, teams may use publicly available, open-source, sample, or realistic synthetic datasets. **All data sources and assumptions must be clearly documented.**

## Expected Deliverables

- Functional prototype or web-based platform presenting a unified view of EV charging infrastructure.
- Interactive national GIS map showing existing chargers, infrastructure coverage, and identified gaps.
- Analytical methodology document explaining the approach used for integration, gap assessment, and estimation.
- Charger requirement estimation and recommendation module for selected states, districts, cities, or transport corridors.
- Decision-support dashboard presenting relevant indicators and planning insights.
- Presentation or demonstration video showcasing the solution for at least one representative geography.
- Scalability roadmap outlining how the solution could be expanded into a nationwide planning platform.
